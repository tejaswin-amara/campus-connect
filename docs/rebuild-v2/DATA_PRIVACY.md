# Student Data Privacy and Retention

## Data minimization
Collect only data required for identity, eligibility, event registration, attendance, security and user-requested notifications. Do not collect demographic, precise location, phone or student identifiers unless the college confirms a specific purpose and policy.

## Data inventory
For each field document purpose, source, sensitivity, lawful/policy basis as confirmed by college, readers, processors, retention period, deletion/anonymization and export behavior.

## Controls
- Role- and event-scoped access to rosters.
- Private storage for tickets and exports.
- Avoid personal data in logs, analytics labels, URLs and notification previews.
- Encrypt transport and managed storage; manage keys outside source.
- Limit exports, record export actor/purpose and expire generated files.
- Define account deactivation, withdrawal, correction and deletion workflows with college policy.
- Define retention for registrations, attendance, audit and security events separately.
- Use synthetic data in tests, Jules sessions, screenshots and demos.

## Legal/policy review
Jules must not invent legal conclusions. College owner must confirm applicable Indian privacy requirements, institutional policy, notices, consent/legitimate-purpose approach, minors if relevant, vendor terms, data residency and breach reporting before production.
